ISO/IEC 18013-5:2021 — diagnostic implementation notes

Scope: these are implementation notes for deterministic diagnostics, not a
replacement for the licensed ISO standard. The nearby file 18013-5.txt contains
ISO/IEC DIS 18013-5:2020(E); it MUST NOT be used to diagnose the 2021 MSO layout.
Do not invent normative clause numbers or infer a complete ISO certification.

Use the 2021 MSO layout: version, digestAlgorithm, valueDigests,
deviceKeyInfo, docType and validityInfo. The version for this diagnostic profile
is "1.0". The holder's public COSE_Key is inside deviceKeyInfo.deviceKey.
valueDigests maps namespace strings directly to maps of digestID to digest bytes.
It does not have an additional "nameSpaces" wrapper. The structure is carried
as encoded CBOR under tag 24 inside the issuerAuth COSE_Sign1 payload.
Issuer namespaces contain tag-24 encoded IssuerSignedItem byte strings; compare
their digests to the corresponding namespace/digestID entries in the MSO.
Undisclosed items may remain in the MSO digest table.

IssuerSigned, Document and DeviceResponse inputs are distinct wrappers.
A credential issued through OIDC4VCI normally contains IssuerSigned; the absence
of deviceAuth at issuance is not an error. Device authentication at presentation
requires the actual SessionTranscript and the MSO-bound device public key.
A verified COSE signature proves possession of the signing key, not authorization
of the issuer, a trusted IACA path, certificate profile compliance or revocation.
Report those aspects as NOT_VERIFIED unless independent evidence is available.

Diagnostic checks supplied with the request are authoritative for the supported
layout, item digests, signature and validity interval. Do not recommend removing
version, moving deviceKey to the MSO root, or wrapping valueDigests in nameSpaces.
Identify optional policy recommendations separately from format errors.
For HAIP, do not recommend including a trust anchor/root in x5chain. A lone
certificate is not automatically a format error; chain requirements depend on
the supplied chain, trust policy and applicable certificate profile.
